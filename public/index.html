Henna Care Tips

Getting a darker stain

Leave the paste on for 6–8 hours if you can. Avoid water for the first 24 hours after removing it — the stain keeps darkening on its own during that time.

Aftercare

Once dry, seal the design with a sugar-lemon mix or a dab of clove/eucalyptus oil. Skip soap and scrubbing on the area for a couple of days.

Choosing a design

Bridal hands can carry heavier, fuller coverage; everyday or festival designs look best minimal — send us a reference photo and we'll adapt it to your hand shape.
