About us

Firdouse Heena is a small independent business run by Firdouse, offering natural, chemical-free henna and the tools henna artists rely on — hand-rolled cones, triple-sifted powder, essential oils, aftercare and practice supplies.

Every batch is prepared fresh and dispatched from Tumkur, Karnataka. We ship across India.

About the founder

Beyond henna, Firdouse is also an engineer and web developer, and has trained many students in web technologies.
